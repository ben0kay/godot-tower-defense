# Laser Tower — sustained Energy beam (Phases 4C-2/4C-3)

Ctrl → Laser Tower → place on route-safe terrain. LaserTowerDefinition registers `laser` in DefaultTowerCatalog; generic placement/HUD handle its 1×1 footprint, private config, prices, selection and upgrades without per-type branches. Initial price 250 Credits, range 294px and 12 base DPS are provisional playtest balance. Level2 costs 175 Credits and directly selects 14 DPS/304px. Battle Tower remains200 Credits with its existing150/250 upgrade prices.

## Combat and retargeting

LaserTowerLevels.cs owns all actual level values: range, float DPS, search/turn/aim, penetration, damage type and independent shield/hull overrides. Its typed one-line rows include construction/upgrade prices. LaserTowerConfig retains non-level identity, priority and tracking policy. Shared TowerTargeting and TurretAiming are reused. Searches start staggered and repeat every configured interval (default .15s), selecting the current best First/Closest candidate. A living valid lock persists between searches; there is no extra cooldown or charge-up. Invalid range/alignment/zero DPS/Enabled=false stops damage immediately. Valid aligned lock sends effective DPS × physics delta as base typed damage to Enemy.ApplyDamage. DamageInfo carries independently optional absolute shield/hull overrides; DamageResolver applies them in place of the corresponding global fallback. Laser defaults to6.00x shield/0.15x hull (72 shield/s or1.8 exposed hull/s at 12 base DPS). Its row flags independently opt out to Energy2.00/0.85; explicit zero is a valid override. Laser contains no defense-resolution math and its authored rows have zero penetration. The selected Inspector shows effective coefficients and base DPS. SetLevelStats replaces one runtime row and emits Changed and refresh the stats/Inspector through the existing cache invalidation route.

One tick damages only its captured lock. Target death/arrival/external unload clears subscriptions and hides the beam immediately; periodic search acquires the next candidate. The fixed placement root never rotates. A child up-facing TurretPivot smoothly tracks, with a Marker2D muzzle at its emitter. No wall line-of-sight, audio, charged shots or critical/status effects are implemented. The shared tower disabling/optional heat foundation exists, but Laser has no numeric heat opt-in in its current level rows.

## Visual ownership and Inspector tuning

BeamRenderer.tscn is one persistent shared renderer with adaptive Outer/Core Line2Ds (up to 28 points), a subtle travelling ripple, smooth cosmetic target-end wander, drawn impact glow and two optional **scene-owned `GpuParticles2D`** emitters. Laser still supplies real muzzle/target world endpoints and active state; gameplay hits remain at the true target. The shared renderer knows no Enemy, targeting service, damage, DPS or wallet. Its style resource `Beam/DefaultLaserBeamStyle.tres` controls colour, widths, original width pulse and the new ripple/wander/glow/particle switches. Reusable spark assets live in `FEEDBACK/Shared/`, not under the Laser Tower. See [beam rendering](../../Shared/Weapons/Beams/README.md) and [Laser preset](Beam/README.md).

Each renderer duplicates its mutable Style on Ready. During play, Remote → World/Towers/LaserTower/BeamRenderer → Style tunes only that visual instance. C#/Remote Inspector property edits refresh its lines, never gameplay caches. Replacing Style unsubscribes the old resource; unloading releases subscriptions. Inactive beams hide and clear endpoints, stop both GPU emitters and cosmetic processing. Edit the saved style for permanent/new-instance defaults. Config and level rows/recipes remain separately copied per tower.

## Earned Rank and Experience

`LaserTowerRanks.cs` sits beside `LaserTowerLevels.cs` and defines all five named ranks in compact explicit rows: Standard0, Veteran1000, Enhanced3000, Superior7000 and Legendary15000 total XP. The selected rank's total bonuses are0/2/4/6/8% damage and0/1/2/3/4% range; future HP columns are inactive. Rank XP is independent of purchased level/prices and is preserved by upgrades. Stage03 modifies the selected complete Level snapshot, never accumulates earlier Rank bonuses. Integer projectile damage retains nearest/ties-to-even rounding; float beams/waves retain fractional damage.

Attacks carry the tower's weak DamageSource. Actual shield/hull loss earns a bounded share of XP derived from each enemy's KillRewardCredits; the lethal source receives kill XP. Shared TowerMasterConfig multipliers default to2× damage pool and10× kill reward. Debug/manual kills and arrivals grant no tower XP. Already-fired payloads retain combat values/source after promotion or upgrade; removed source towers do not receive XP. The bottom-right selected Inspector shows Rank, within-rank/total XP, progress bar, active bonuses and MAX RANK automatically through events. See TOWERS/Shared/Ranks/README.md for exact rules and regression results.

## Live ammunition supply

Current complete LaserTowerLevels.cs rows include 1000 reserve / 2 per second of funded beam firing. Defaults are provisional except Battle1000/1. InitialAmmoFraction starts full by default; empty reserves stop unpaid attacks, and paid levels/ranks never refill them. The existing Inspector displays current ammo/delivery state and changed next-level ammo values. Factory/depot drones deliver physical cargo only on arrival. See `TOWERS/Shared/Ammunition/README.md` and `TESTS/Economy/Ammunition.md`.
