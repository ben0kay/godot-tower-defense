# Drone Station
The 2×3 Utility building lives here: scripts, scene, config, stable `drone_station` definition, construction/artwork and station roster UI. Fighter ownership/AI, artwork and `FighterDroneManager` remain in [DRONES/Combat](../../../DRONES/Combat/README.md).

Complete construction, BUY a fighter (250 Credits), choose its slot and LAUNCH into Auto-pilot. RECALL returns it to its own dock. Default capacity is two; purchases, HP/ammo and ownership persist without free refills. Disabled stations recall fighters; selling/destruction removes the owned roster.

FD1-B and FD2 are source implemented, awaiting owner validation. Building cost/footprint/dock markers are unchanged. Weapons, patrol UI and ammunition/repair service remain future phases.
