# Industrial Generator — FUTURE / NOT IMPLEMENTED

**Role:** the reliable, affordable, **standard starting generator used on most Tower Defense maps**. Medium electricity output: more practical for the main installation than tiny Solar, but significantly below expensive endgame Fusion.

Like all generators it contributes electricity to its entire edge-connected terrain island automatically. A manual pair of Power Relays may join that island to others, creating one shared supply/demand network. No wires, radius-based supply or individual tower assignments.

Proposed tunables: output, construction cost/work time, structural durability, and eventual research efficiency/output. Exact capacity/cost and possible fuel requirements are **unsettled**; do not make fuel logistics mandatory in the first pass. The building has no mandatory targeting/ammo/weapon logic.

See [Solar Generator](../SolarGenerator/README.md), [Fusion Reactor](../FusionReactor/README.md) and [POWER](../../../POWER/README.md). Later tests should cover multiple generators, grid merges/splits, loss of power and saves.

**Documentation only; not implemented.**
