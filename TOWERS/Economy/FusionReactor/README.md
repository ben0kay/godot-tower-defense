# Fusion Reactor — FUTURE / NOT IMPLEMENTED

**Role:** expensive, very high-output **endgame** electricity generation for large defensive installations. It is not the default generator at the start of an ordinary level.

Proposed tunables: very high output, large construction/upgrade costs and time, optional footprint/health, future research efficiency and possible late-game operating constraints. Fuel, risk, vulnerability and exact values remain open choices; do not invent those mechanics during the initial power pass.

Fusion contributes electricity to the grid on its own connected terrain island. It cannot power remote asteroid islands across empty space unless their grids are joined with a **manually connected pair of relays**. Higher-value infrastructure creates something worth protecting, while local consumption/priority rules remain shared.

Generation and network calculations belong to [POWER](../../../POWER/README.md); individual building data remains here. Avoid per-building grid scans and redundant network managers.

**Future notes only. No reactor code, art or scene.**
