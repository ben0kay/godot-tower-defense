# Solar Generator — FUTURE / NOT IMPLEMENTED

**Role:** cheap, compact, sustainable **low-output** generator especially suited to a small isolated asteroid platform, where building a full power bridge is unnecessary.

Its electricity contributes automatically to the shared grid of its **connected terrain island**. A remote island with no relay links stays independent; if manually paired relays bridge it to another island, its solar output becomes part of the merged grid too. No per-generator local-only/export mode and no cables within an island.

Future tunables: small power output, construction cost/time, footprint, durability and research output modifiers. Exact numbers are not chosen; it should stay distinctly weaker and cheaper than [Industrial Generator](../IndustrialGenerator/README.md) and [Fusion Reactor](../FusionReactor/README.md).
