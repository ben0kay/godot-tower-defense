# Tiles — Source Art

Tile art is reusable across many levels. Keep texture/atlas images here, grouped by material or visual purpose.

Artwork will initially be simple 64×64 placeholders and can later be swapped for detailed hand-painted sprites. Do not use colors to determine passability: a TileSet's custom-data rules define gameplay.

- `Metal/`: sci-fi base construction surfaces and walls
- `Dirt/`: natural/rocky material and worn earth textures
- `Decorative/`: surface decals and purely visual tile details

Large nebulae, planets and starfields belong to `WORLD/BACKGROUNDS/` instead.
